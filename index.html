A table for every occasion

Prime Plate is a modern, luxury family restaurant on Matara Road in Kamburupitiya. We cook Sri Lankan rice and curry, kottu and fried rice, and we bake pizza and grill burgers in our own Pizza Corner. Our guests tell us the food is delicious, the prices fair and the staff friendly.
